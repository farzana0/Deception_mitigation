Extraction: the existing 40 paired scenarios only; post-block residual outputs.
CAA: per-completion mean over non-special response tokens, then mean honest-
dishonest paired differences. Candidate zero-based layers: 8,12,16,20,24.
Joint: paired residual predictor-state differences in position bins 0,1-3,4-11,12+;
position zero is the last prompt token. Stack layer x bin rows, rank-2 SVD.
Normalize each bin by sqrt(sum_l (||delta_l||/typical_residual_norm_l)^2).
Thus CAA and joint strengths are comparable total relative perturbations per
prediction step. Pooled uses fixed CAA directions at the same joint layers,
with the same total budget and the locked joint strength.

StTP: L2 logistic regression per layer, C=1, lbfgs, honest positive, no feature
standardization. Unit direction w/||w||, threshold -b/||w||, honest projection
mean and sample standard deviation. Gate p<t, then target mu_pos+c*sd_pos.
StTP is not rescaled after this formula; realized strengths are reported.

Calibration: the 10 reserved scenarios only, with neutral system prompts.
Choose by mean length-normalized honest-minus-dishonest completion log likelihood.
CAA/joint grid: .02,.05,.10; StTP c grid: 0,1,2. No MASK or Machiavelli results
were used in choosing settings. Locked at 2026-11-06T02:48:18Z:
